# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention and disclosure of another
person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through GitHub.
Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where possible, consider the context,
and may remove content, warn participants or restrict participation. Maintainers
are accountable to these same expectations. Good-faith reports will not result in
retaliation. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces where someone represents this project.
